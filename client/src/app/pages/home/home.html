The Challenge

Nearly 12 million children in the U.S. live in poverty

Many lack access to the education, guidance, and basic resources they need to succeed. About 1 in every 6 children faces homelessness, foster care, or financial instability in a single-parent household.

We believe there is a shared social responsibility to lift these students up — and we focus on creating meaningful impact one child at a time.

🎯 Our Mission

Connect underserved students with academic support and life skills to help them succeed in school, career, and life.

🔭 Our Vision

Empower underprivileged students across Tampa Bay to achieve their full potential through education and opportunity.

Why It Matters

Education is the most powerful path out of poverty

When a student gets consistent support, everything changes — grades, confidence, and what they believe is possible.
